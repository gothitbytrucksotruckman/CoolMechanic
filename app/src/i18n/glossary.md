# Glossary — owner-facing words

Owner screens avoid refrigeration jargon (PRD §9.2). Use the right-hand word in UI copy.

| Technical term | Owner word (ID) | EN |
|---|---|---|
| kondensor (condenser) | kisi-kisi belakang | back coils |
| evaporator | bagian pendingin di dalam | cooling part inside |
| refrigeran / freon | freon | refrigerant |
| kompresor | kompresor (mesin di bawah belakang) | compressor (the motor at the lower back) |
| gasket | karet pintu | door seal |
| pipa discharge | pipa buang kompresor | compressor outlet pipe |
| arus RMS | arus listrik | current |
| getaran RMS | getaran | vibration |
